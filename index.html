Samsara Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Samsara mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

Samsara may collect the following categories of data:

  • Account information, such as email address, display name, and sign-in provider details.
  • User-generated content, including intentions, dialogue history, journal entries, and gameplay progress.
  • Technical and analytics data, such as app usage events, crash-related diagnostics, and interaction statistics.
  • Advertising-related data required for displaying ads and measuring ad performance.
  • Audio-related permissions and temporary microphone input when the user chooses voice input features.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide core app features and maintain gameplay progress.
  • Enable account sign-in and cloud synchronization.
  • Generate and improve AI-guided dialogue experiences.
  • Support journal export, onboarding, localization, and audio features.
  • Measure app quality, engagement, and feature performance.
  • Serve advertising and rewarded ad flows where applicable.

3. Third-Party Services

Samsara may use third-party providers, including but not limited to:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics).
  • Google Sign-In.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob.
  • AI and voice providers used for dialogue and audio features.

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage and Retention

User data may be stored locally on the device and, if the user signs in or cloud sync is enabled, in secure cloud services used by the app.

We retain data only for as long as necessary to provide app functionality, improve the service, comply with legal obligations, or until the user requests deletion.

5. Microphone and Audio

Samsara may request microphone access only when the user explicitly uses voice input. Audio input is used to support the requested feature and is not recorded continuously in the background.

6. Advertising

The app may display banner ads, rewarded ads, interstitial ads, or native ads. Advertising partners may collect device or usage data as needed to provide ad services, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

7. Children’s Privacy

Samsara is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law in their region. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data associated with your account.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing where applicable.
