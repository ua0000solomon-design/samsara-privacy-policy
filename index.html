Samsara Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

1. Overview

Samsara is a reflective mobile game inspired by Lila. The app includes a game board, journal, AI dialogue with the Sage, optional voice features, authentication, analytics, and advertising. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as display name, email address, and profile photo when you sign in with Google or email.
  • Gameplay data, including current cell, dice rolls, progress, puzzle attempts, completed levels, and premium/free status.
  • Dialogue and journal content that you choose to enter, including intentions, reflections, and messages exchanged with the AI Sage.
  • Voice input text when you use microphone-based speech recognition.
  • Analytics events, such as app opens, screen usage, dice rolls, audio settings changes, ad events, and feature usage.
  • Device and advertising data processed by Google AdMob, such as advertising identifiers and ad interaction signals.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Run the game and save your progress.
  • Provide AI dialogue, cell explanations, and reflective prompts.
  • Store journal entries and chat history if cloud sync is available.
  • Improve game balance, prompts, safety flows, audio, and user experience.
  • Provide optional voice output and voice input features.
  • Show ads, measure ad performance, and support rewarded ad features.
  • Detect crisis-related text and show safety information inside the app.

4. AI, Voice, and Sensitive Content

The Sage dialogue is generated by an AI service. Your messages, intention, current game position, dice result, and relevant cell meaning may be sent to an AI provider to generate a response. If voice narration is enabled, text may be sent to a text-to-speech provider to generate audio.

Samsara is a reflective entertainment experience. It is not medical, psychological, legal, financial, or crisis counseling. If you are in danger or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that process data under their own policies:

  • Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Core by Google.
  • Google Sign-In.
  • Google AdMob for banner, native, rewarded, and rewarded interstitial ads.
  • Anthropic Claude or another AI provider for AI dialogue.
  • ElevenLabs or another text-to-speech provider for voice narration.
  • Speech recognition services available on your device or platform.

You can review Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Advertising

Samsara may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interaction data, and other information to provide, limit, measure, and personalize ads where permitted by law and your settings. Rewarded ads are optional and are shown only when you choose to receive an in-app reward.

7. Microphone and Audio

Microphone access is used only when you start voice input. The app uses recognized speech text to fill your message field or send your response to the Sage. You can disable microphone access in your device settings.

8. Data Storage and Security

Some data may be stored locally on your device. If you sign in, progress, journal entries, and chat history may be synced to cloud storage. We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no system is guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You may:

  • Use the app as a guest where supported.
  • Disable music, sound effects, voice, or silence mode in settings.
  • Disable microphone permission in device settings.
  • Choose not to watch rewarded ads.
  • Delete or reset local journey progress using app settings where available.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of cloud-stored data linked to your account.

10. Children

Samsara is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the latest version became effective.
