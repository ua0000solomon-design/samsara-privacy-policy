Samsara Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: June 4, 2026

How to Request Deletion

To delete your Samsara account and associated data, send an email to ua0000solomon@gmail.com from the email address connected to your Samsara account.

Please use this subject line:

Samsara account deletion request

If you signed in with Google, please send the request from the same Google account email used in the app. This helps us verify that the request comes from the account owner.

Data We Delete

After verifying your request, we will delete account-linked data stored by Samsara, including:

  • Account profile data stored by the app, such as email, display name, and user identifier.
  • Game progress, current journey state, dice history, puzzle progress, and settings stored in cloud sync.
  • Chat history with the Sage, intentions, reflections, and journal entries stored in cloud sync.
  • Analytics data that can reasonably be connected to your app account, where deletion is technically available.

Data That May Remain

Some data may remain where required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or where it is stored only in aggregated or anonymized form. Data processed by third-party services, such as Google AdMob, Firebase, or authentication providers, may also be subject to their own retention and deletion processes.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. We may contact you if we need additional information to verify the account.

Local Device Data

You can also remove local app data by deleting the Samsara app from your device or clearing app storage in your device settings. Cloud-stored data still requires a deletion request by email.
